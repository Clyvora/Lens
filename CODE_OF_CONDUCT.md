# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Clyvora Lens to be a welcoming project where people can contribute without harassment or intimidation. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and accept constructive feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in the repository and in public spaces when someone is representing the project. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainers or GitHub's reporting tools. Do not include more personal information than is necessary. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and fairly as practical.

This code of conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and is intentionally kept concise for this project.
